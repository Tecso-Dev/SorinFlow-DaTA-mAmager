# Code of Conduct — منشور رفتاری

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to do good work.

## What is expected

- Give feedback on the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming a mistake was carelessness.
- Accept that people work in different languages and time zones. This project is
  Persian-first; English is welcome too, and neither is a second-class citizen.
- When you are wrong, say so plainly and move on. Nobody needs an apology essay.

## What is not

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Deliberately wasting people's time: bad-faith arguments, spam, or reports you
  have not checked.
- Sharing credentials, customer phone numbers, or scraped personal data in an
  issue, a pull request, or a comment. This project handles real people's
  contact details — treat them the way you would want yours treated.

## Reporting

Raise it with the maintainer directly: sobhan.gjhav.azimzadeh@gmail.com.
Reports are handled privately.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that do
not follow this document, and may block accounts for repeated or serious
behaviour.

## Scope

Applies in this repository and anywhere someone is representing the project.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
shortened deliberately: a document nobody reads protects nobody.
